About the Department

The Electronics and Communication Engineering department started in the year 1981. The department has a rich history of academic excellence and has consistently produced graduates who excel in their careers. With focus on practical learning and industry collaboration, the department aims to equip students with the skills and knowledge needed to thrive in the ever-evolving field of electronics and communication engineering.

About E-Manthana

E-Manthana is the annual technical fest of the Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) Department at the Adichunchanagiri Institute of Technology (AIT), Chikkamagaluru. E-Manthana serves as a platform for innovation, learning, and competition among engineering students. It features technical paper presentations, circuit design contests, project exhibitions, coding challenges, and other engineering-based activities that encourage the practical application of classroom concepts. Join us for an exciting cultural event filled with vibrant performances and creative expressions. Inspiring guest speakers will also share valuable insights and experiences to enlighten the audience. Together, these events promote both technical excellence and holistic development among students at AIT. E-Manthana, which began in 2014, has now successfully completed 15 glorious years of inspiring innovation and technical excellence.
